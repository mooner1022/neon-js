NetOptions

class NetOptions

What the fetch and WebSocket of a context may reach, and how much of the host they may use. Nothing is reachable by default: a host names the hosts its scripts need (Builder.allowHosts). Whatever the hosts, a connection to a loopback, private, link-local or otherwise non-public address is refused unless its range is allowed (Builder.allowAddresses), and so is cleartext (http:, ws:) unless allowed (Builder.allowInsecure).

client
Link copied to clipboard
val client: OkHttpClient?

The host's client, whose connection pool, dispatcher, DNS, TLS settings and interceptors are used (default: a new one). The policy is applied on a client derived from it: redirects, caches, cookies and proxies are not used, and connections go through NeonJS's socket factory, which enforces the address rules. Its dispatcher's limits apply too (OkHttp's default runs 5 requests per host at once, and queues the others).
